1. Overview

Hastelloy X (UNS N06002, W.Nr. 2.4665) is a nickel-based superalloy that combines excellent high-temperature strength with outstanding resistance to oxidizing, carburizing and reducing atmospheres. It retains good ductility after long-term exposure to temperatures up to 1200 °C (2200 °F) and offers excellent formability and weldability, making it a preferred material for forged gas turbine and aerospace components.

4. Open-Die Forging Capabilities

Jiangyin Jiangnan Metal Co., Ltd. produces Hastelloy X open-die forgings in the following forms:

  • Forged rings — seamless rolled rings and contour rings
