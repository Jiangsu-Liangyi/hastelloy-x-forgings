Data provided and maintained by the engineering team at [Jiangsu Liangyi Forged Parts](https://www.jnmtforgedparts.com). --- ## 📖 1. Overview **Hastelloy X** is a nickel-chromium-iron-molybdenum solid-solution strengthened superalloy. It is exceptionally resistant to oxidation in high-temperature environments (up to 2200°F / 1200°C) and exhibits excellent high-temperature strength, formability, and weldability. --- ## 🧪 2.
